S. E. Asia

January/February 2025

Monday 13th January Once again, our holiday started with me having to go for a PET scan. We left Kingsclere at midday and drove to Windsor. I was booked in for my scan at GenesisCare at 14:00 and we had booked a room at the Holiday Inn Express which was conveniently located next door. GenesisCare had said we could leave the car in their car park overnight so we parked there and then went and checked into the hotel. I had an hour to kill before my scan so walked into Windsor with Roz. She then went off to meet Aleks for coffee and I walked back to GenesisCare.

The scan all went OK. As with my previous PET can, it was a fairly boring couple of hours but everything was on time and I was out again by 16:00. I walked back into Windsor and met Roz and Alex in the Wetherspoons (The King and Castle). I had a pint in there while waiting for them to finish their coffees. Aleks then went home leaving us to go round a few pubs.

We left Aleks at the train station and went for a pint in The Windsor Castle. From there we walked across the river to Eton. The pubs there were either closed or lacking in decent beer options so we returned to Windsor and walked up to the Carpenters Arms for one. We went from there to The Two Brewers, a pub we had not been in before. This was probably the nicest pub we have been to in Windsor. We had a couple in there before walking back down the hill to Zizzi's where we had a couple of pizzas and a bottle of wine.

We decided that we should be reasonably sensible at this point and instead of going to more pubs, went back to the hotel where we had another couple of glasses of wine and a relatively early night.

Tuesday 14th January We were up by 6:30, had showers and checked out of the hotel. It was a drive of about 25 minutes to Heathrow, Terminal 4 where we had a wait of about 20 minutes before our meet and great driver turned up. We checked in, got rid of our luggage and were through security with plenty of time for our 10:25 Malaysian Airlines flight to KL. The flight departed on time and was not totally full so we had a bit more space than usual.

As with last year, the service was pretty good and we were given our first glasses of wine shortly after take-off. They kept the wine coming but we stopped drinking about half way through the flight so did not get too pissed. We had a meal a couple of hours into the flight and breakfast at about 4am Malaysian time. The flight landed at KLIA on time just before 7.

Wednesday 15th January KLIA was as bad as ever but we did get through the airport relatively quickly this year. We had to get on a bus from the satellite bit as the train is no longer running. We then had to clear immigration before getting our next flight to Langkawi. You are supposed to fill out an electronic form before getting to the immigration desks but the form is just about impossible to fill in using a phone. However, there were no queues at the desks and one of immigration officials just took all our details and stamped us into the country without bothering with the form. By 7:45 we were sat at the gate waiting for our next flight.

The connecting flight was also on time and we landed in Langkawi at 10:00. We had a bit of a wait for our luggage but by 10:30, we were outside at the taxi booth. We got a taxi straight to the Mercure at Cenang beach and arrived there by about 10:45.

There was a bit of confusion about which room we were getting and after a bit of discussion, we agreed to stay in a high up room for one night and then move to one of the poolside rooms tomorrow. Today's room was not ready yet so we went across the road to Ali's Bistro for our first roti canai of the trip. We bought a couple of bottles of wine from the duty free shop and returned to the hotel. We were told now that we would have two nights in the first room and move to a ground floor room on Friday but at least the first room was now ready.

We were given a room on the 7th floor facing away from the sea. This was probably a nicer room than the ground floor rooms but lacks the convenience of being by the pool. We were both totally knackered by this time so had showers and then fell asleep. I had not got any sleep on the flight and Roz had not slept much so although we did not want to sleep for too long, a couple of hours was needed.

By the time we woke up, the weather had deteriorated and it looked as if it had rained or was about to rain. We did both venture out at about 4. Roz went to the pool and I went for a short walk to complete my 10,000 steps for the day.

We had not been feeling great when we arrived but after our sleep and a bit of exercise, we were just about back to feeling 100%. We drank one of the bottles of wine sat on our balcony while trying to come up with some sort of plan for the four days in between leaving here and getting to Nai Yang. It is looking as if a ferry to Ko Lanta via Ko Lipe may be the best option.

We went out to eat at Viva Italia where we had eaten last year a few times. We had a few cocktails and food from the Indian upstairs. The weather had not been great since we arrived here and by the time we had finished eating, it was raining heavily. We left the restaurant and made it as far as Cinnamons Bar and had a few glasses of wine while waiting for the rain to clear. Eventually made it back to the hotel having had slightly more alcohol than we had planned. We had one more drink on the balcony before bed.

Thursday 16th January Neither of us were too badly hungover so we were able to get up and go across the road to Ali's for breakfast. We had a quick look at the beach and asked in a couple of travel agents about ferry tickets to Lanta. No-one seemed to be able to sell them and our banana shake place was closed so we returned to the hotel empty-handed.

The weather was pretty poor again today but it was sunny enough to go the pool for an hour once back at the hotel. We left when the rain started then went back for another 30 minutes or so but gave up on sunbathing after the second shower. This was probably long enough in the sun for our first day here anyway.

We went out and got banana shakes at lunchtime and it was just about dry enough to sit on our balcony to drink them. I went to the gym in the afternoon and managed a 5km run. Roz went for a swim in the rain.

We had planned to eat at Royale India tonight as we were not drinking but it was closed due to some sort of private function. Instead we returned to Ali's and had very good currys again. Back in the room by 8:30.

Friday 17th January We were up early again so went over the road to Ali's for breakfast. We asked at reception about moving rooms and as usual, there was a lot of confusion. We went and sat by the pool for an hour and by 10:45, they had sorted it out and we were able to move into room 014, one of the rooms with pool access. It is exactly the same as the room we were in last year and just round the corner from it.

We had another 30 minutes or so by the pool as the weather was better today than it had been so far on the trip, and then went into Kuah. We got a taxi from outside the hotel and were dropped at Eagle Square near the ferry terminal. We went straight into the terminal and bought tickets for the 9:15 ferry to Ko Lipe on Monday.

We had a wander round the eagle statue and then walked a mile or so through a slightly odd park to the Maha Tower. We had thought about going up it but it was getting a bit cloudy by this time and neither of us were that enthusiastic any more. Instead we walked into the centre of Kuah trying to work out if there was anything we recognised from when we were here in 1990. We eventually gave up on this and got a Grab taxi back to Chenang.

We had banana shakes on our new balcony and tried to book accommodation in Lanta. However, the place that we were both keen on had sold out since yesterday which was a big disappointment. It also started raining again. Roz went for another swim and I did another 5km on the treadmill.

We had another evening similar to Wednesday. We started on the wine on our balcony at 7ish and got through a bottle before walking to Viva Italia. We had another very good Indian meal in there with a few cocktails before going to Cinnamons Bar for a couple of glasses of wine. We had a couple more drinks back at the room and got to bed sometime after midnight.

Saturday 18th January Breakfast at Ali's followed by a few hours by the pool. I went for a walk at 1ish. I took out money and changed some of it into Thai Baht as we will need this to enter Thailand on Monday. I also bought nibbles and wine for tonight and returned to the pool with our lunchtime banana shakes.

We managed to book accommodation for our stay in Lanta. It is all a bit unclear as to how good this would be but we seemed to be running out of options so needed to get something booked. No rain this afternoon so we were able to return to the pool again after getting this done. Once again, at 3:30ish, I went for a run in the gym and Roz swam in the pool.

We had a slightly earlier start to the session tonight as I was hoping that there may be a chance of watching some of the Cardiff v Swansea game somewhere. We had a beer and a few glasses of wine on our balcony before walking to Viva Italia. The curries were particularly good tonight, especially the squid vindaloo.

I had a couple of cocktails again while Roz stayed on the wine. We stopped on the way back at Cinnamons by which time Swansea had started conceding goals so I had lost interest in going back to try and watch the game. We had a few glasses of wine and then stopped at the duty free shop to buy more to drink on the balcony. An enjoyable night again.

Sunday 19th January I was slightly more hungover this morning than on previous mornings but we were still up in time for breakfast at Ali Bistro. We spent a couple of hours by the pool and then went for a walk along the beach stopping to buy our banana shakes on the way back.

We did not do much for the rest of the afternoon except lay around by the pool or sit on the balcony. My 5km run was hard work today due to the amount we have drunk the last two nights but I just about made it.

We were not drinking tonight as we are travelling tomorrow and needed a night off anyway. Royale India was open again so we ate there and had another superb meal. The food in Langkawi has definitely been a highlight of our stay here again. We were both totally knackered by the time we finished eating so went straight back to the hotel for an early night.

Monday 20th January We were woken by the alarm at 6:30 and checked out of the hotel by 7:00. We ordered a Grab taxi which took about 15 minutes to arrive and we were back at Kuah Ferry Pier just after check-in opened at 7:45. We had to check in and get boarding passes as well as being given our arrival cards for Thailand. It all seemed a bit chaotic but seemed to work quite efficiently and about 10 minutes after getting there we were leaving with all our paperwork.... and our stickers.

We found a restaurant in the terminal building that did Malaysian food so were able to get our roti canais before wandering down to the departures area. Boarding started at about 8:30. Again this all seemed a bit disorganised and immigration was very slow but we had managed to get close to the front of the queue and were through and on the boat relatively quickly. Surprisingly the ferry pulled out exactly on time at 9:15.

It was a two hour journey to Ko Lipe where we arrived at 10:15 Thai time. We were taken off the ferry by long-tail boats and then had to queue to get our passports back (we had had to hand them in before getting on the ferry). This was another slow process as there was only one person distributing the passports and she was also selling boat tickets to onward destinations. This did not work out too badly for us as we wanted to buy boat tickets and had been in the first long-tail so again, were close to the front of the queue. We were able to buy tickets for the 12:30 speedboat to Lanta, got our passports back and proceeded to immigration.

Immigration was very quick due to the bottle-neck at the previous stage. We got our passports stamped, paid our national park fees, collected our luggage and were away from all the chaos by 10:30. We walked along the beach to where the next ferry departed and found a restaurant where we could sit looking at the beach while waiting for the speedboat. This also left on time. We had a brief delay as we ran out of petrol just outside Saladan but this was resolved quickly and we were at the Saladan pier by about 15:30 which was as good as we could possibly have hoped for at the start of the day.

We started walking towards Salatan Resort where we had booked a room for tonight. We eventually got a tuk-tuk that would take us for a reasonable price and we were checked into our room before 16:00. It is all a bit basic compared to staying at the Mercure but it is in a nice location and everything seems to work. Lanta seems to be very busy at the moment and we had not been able to get anywhere we wanted for four consecutive nights. We will have one night here before moving on for the rest of our stay here.

We spent an hour or so by the pool and then went down to the beach in front of the pool to watch the sunset. We then walked back along the beach into Saladan and through the grounds of the hotel we had wanted to book initially for our stay here. We took out money and walked round the bit of town we had not seen yet before going to eat at Lanta Seafood. We had a very enjoyable meal there before walking back to the resort for another early night.

Tuesday 21st January There was a free breakfast included with our room. Not a huge selection but there was tea and toast so enough to keep us going. We had to check out this morning but we quite like the pool area here so spent an hour or so by the pool before checking out at 11:45.

The hotel we had booked for the next three nights was Coco Cape, a mile away just North of Kaw Kwang. We got there just after midday and were shown straight to our room. We had been a bit unsure what to expect when we booked a room here but it turned out to be an excellent choice. We had booked a "Honeymoon Suite with Sea View". The room itself was very nice looking out over the Northern Tip of the island and the balcony was excellent. It is totally private with plenty of space, an outdoor shower and a spa bath. The swimming pool had looked very nice as we walked past it but it was also very crowded so I think we could be spending quite a lot of the next three days on the balcony!

After spending an hour or so unpacking and generally sorting ourselves out, we went out for a walk. We wanted to see what had happened to Kaw Kwang Beach Resort. We had stayed there when we first came to Lanta in 2000 and it is probably the place we have stayed in more than any other in Thailand. We knew it did not appear to exist any more but thought it may have been taken over by an upmarket resort.

As it was, it was still there in the same place but completely overgrown. The buildings were all still there and we saw one of the owners in what used to be the restaurant. He told us that it closed three years ago and is now up for sale. It was a strange feeling wandering round the grounds seeing the rooms we had stayed in so often still with furniture on the balconies, and seeing it all so overgrown. All quite sad but at least it was all still there and hopefully it can be sold and restored.... although it will probably be sold and turned into another luxury resort.

We walked through the resort to the beach, then started looking for something for lunch. We walked past Twin Bay Resort and then bought food from one of the street stalls in the road there. We ate on the wall by the sea there and it was a surprisingly tasty meal. After banana shakes on the beach, we walked back to Coco Cape.

We spent most of the rest of the afternoon on the balcony including an hour or so in the bath spa. At 4:30 I went for a run along the beach. It was a bit tougher than running on a treadmill in an air-conditioned gym but I just about managed my 5km again. Roz was in the pool when I got back and I spent about 15 minutes in it trying to cool down. Coco Cape is a very good place for watching the sunsetting. There are good views from the bar, the restaurant and the pool and also from our balcony. The view from our shower is also not bad.

We were not really sure where to go and eat in Lanta as it is seven years since we were here last and just about all the places we used to eat are no longer there. We decided to try the Coco Cape restaurant and the food was very good again. Another quiet night and we were back in the room by 8:30

Wednesday 22nd January We did not have breakfast included here so we settled for tea and biscuits on the balcony for breakfast. It is a lovely place to sit although watching all the dive boats going out for the day was all a bit sad again. At 9ish, Roz went to the pool and I walked into Saladan. I had a wander around, made a few inquiries about taxis for Friday and bought a few things for tonight. After walking back to the resort, I joined Roz by the pool for an hour.

We returned to where the food stalls were yesterday for lunch but the stalls had not yet appeared so we returned to the resort restaurant for lunch. By 1:30, we were back on the balcony. I went for my run at about 4:15. It was slightly cooler today so it was a bit easier than yesterday and I managed to run all the way this time.

Today was the 35th anniversary of our first date in Sydney 35 years ago. We usually try and go for a pizza on this day but after a lot of dithering decided to stick with Thai food. We started drinking on the balcony at 6:30 while watching the sunset. We drank a bottle of the wine we had bought from Langkawi and then got a tuk-tuk into Saladan where we booked a taxi to take us to Nai Yang on Friday. We did look at a couple of pizza options but neither appealed that much so we returned to Lanta Seafood.

We had very good meals again with our first Sang Som set of the trip. We got another tuk-tuk back to the resort buying a large bottle of Sang Som on the way. Back at the resort, we managed to lock ourselves on the balcony somehow and had to get someone from reception to come up and rescue us. Once this was resolved, we drank just over half the bottle before getting to bed sometime around midnight.

Thursday 23rd January We did not do a lot all day today. We have both felt the effects of last nights alcohol with Roz probably more badly affected than me.

I went for a walk into Saladan at 10ish but had to go back to sleep when I got back to the room. We both made it to lunch in the restaurant at about 1:30 but Roz could only manage half a sandwich before returning to the room. We did make it briefly to the pool in the evening but a lot of the day was spent lazing around in the room or on the balcony.

Neither of us could be bothered to go too far in the evening to eat so we had our second meal of the day in the resort restaurant and were back in the room by 8.

Friday 24th January We were up earlier today and both back to feeling 100%. After our morning tea, I walked the full length of the beach while Roz had coffee on the balcony and then went for a swim. Surprisingly, at this time of morning the beach was quite busy with loads of people walking, running and various fitness activities. It was a nice way to start the day.

After showers, we checked out at 10:50 and went and had breakfast/lunch in the restaurant. We had booked a taxi for 11:30 and the car was there by the time we had finished eating. The drive of just over four hours was quite enjoyable. It was a very comfortable car and it did not feel like a four hour journey. There was no sign of the bridge joining Lanta to the mainland having been started so we were still on the old ferry for that bit of the trip but everything else was fairly smooth. We arrived at Nai Yang Beach Resort at 15:20.

After the traditional argument about an unwanted room upgrade, we were given room 8310 in "The Sea Turtle" block. We have not stayed in this block for a few years but have always quite liked the rooms here. We were given one of the rooms on the top floor, which are not as good in my opinion but it was in a good position with a better view than the last time we were here. All in all we were both quite happy with it.

We went out for a walk around and to buy some things for tonight before returning to the balcony with banana shakes. I went back out to buy the alcohol just after 5.

We started drinking on the balcony at about 6:45. We drank a bottle of wine before heading out. One of the two restaurants we ate in a lot last year was closed but the other one, Rotcharin Seafood, was still there so we ate there. We had one bottle of Sang Som with us and inevitably, I went to get another when we finished that one. We stayed drinking on the beach after the restaurant had closed and it was another very enjoyable evening although neither of us remember too much about walking back to the hotel.

Saturday 25th January We woke up with no repeat of the hangovers we had a couple of days ago. We were able to get up and go for breakfast (included) and then spend a few hours by the pool albeit in the shade.

I went for a walk at about 1:30 and met Roz to buy lunch on the way back. Had another hour or so by the pool in the afternoon. I went out to stock up on Sang Som and water again at 5ish but that was about it for today.

We had a similar evening to yesterday. We had a few extra drinks on the balcony while watching the first half of Norwich v Swansea. We could not find anywhere showing football (although we did not try that hard) so went to eat at the restaurant further along the beach from where we were last night. After finishing our Sang Som, we returned to the room to watch the second half of the Sunderland game. We managed to resist the temptation to go out and buy more alcohol when we ran out completely so went to bed when the football finished.

Sunday 26th January Another quiet day. We had breakfast then Roz went to the pool while I went for a walk. I walked further along the beach than I had been before and got as far as the airport runway. I spent about 30 minutes on the beach there with a few hundred other people all taking photos of the planes coming in just above our heads.

Did not do much else for the rest of the day. It was quite similar to a lot of Sundays. We had lunch on the balcony consisting of banana shakes and kit-kats. Spent the rest of the afternoon by the pool or in the room. Neither of us were particularly hungover today but we were both quite knackered by the evening. We were not bothered about going out to eat so got a couple of pizzas from Lee's across the road. We ate them on the balcony and had an early night.

Monday 27th January Another quiet day. After breakfast, I walked round to the airport and back before joining Roz at the pool. I did not want to spend too much time in the sun today as I got burnt walking on the beach yesterday, so spent most of the rest of the day in the room or on the balcony.

We had left over pizza for lunch. During the afternoon, we applied for our Cambodian e-visas. We were a few dollars short of being able to get visas on arrival and this was probably a more sensible option anyway but it was disappointing to miss out on the chance to get the visas in our passports!

We did made it out briefly in the evening. We booked a taxi to take us to the airport tomorrow and bought another take-away from over the road which we ate on the balcony.

Tuesday 28th January A rare healthy start to the day. I was up by 6:45 and went out for a 5km run. Roz went to the pool for a swim. After we had both finished, we went to breakfast and then had a couple of hours to kill before checking out. Roz went back to the pool. I stayed in the room.

We went down to reception at 11:10 and were checked out remarkably quickly so had a 15 minute wait for our taxi. Just as the taxi was turning up, we found out that our 13:20 flight had been delayed by an hour. We were at the airport by about 11:40, checked in my rucksack, collected boarding passes and went through security to wait.

Our plane eventually appeared at about 14:30 and departed just after 15:00. The flight time was just over 1 hour 20 minutes. We approached Don Muang from the South which was unusual but meant that we had good views of central Bangkok as we came in. We were taken by bus to the terminal, a journey that took far longer than it should have done. However, I got my luggage fairly quickly and there were no queues at all for the taxis so by 17:00, we were on our way into Bangkok. For the first time we can remember, the taxi did not use the expressway as there was not that much traffic. It was still heavy round Victory Monument but we were at the hotel before 18:00.

We are in Bangkok for two nights and have one night booked at The Ibis and one night at The Mercure. The Ibis was quite a bit cheaper and we will not need the added benefits of the Mercure (i.e. gym and pool) tonight. We checked in, had showers and then went across to the MBK.

After an unsuccessful look for new running shoes, we went down to Ban Khun Mae which was in the same location as last year. We had a bit of a wait until we got a table in the only nice part of the restaurant but once inside, the food was as good as ever. After eating we had a bit of a wander round looking for somewhere to get passport photos but a lot of the places were closed so we gave up and returned to the hotel.

Wednesday 29th January We were up early again this morning and, after our cups of tea, headed out to do our sight-seeing. The only thing we had planned to visit was Wat Arun. Neither of us had been there before. We nearly went in last year as we ended up at the ferry docking point there but it was too hot and there were far too many people around so we did not bother going in.

This time, we got a Grab taxi from the hotel (฿140) and were there as it opened at 8am. This was a very good sight and a very good time to go and see it. We were some of the first people in so there was hardly anyone around as we went round for the first time. The main tower itself is very spectacular and the surroundings also good. We spent about an hour wandering round taking pictures before going outside the complex for breakfast.

By the time we went back in, the crowds were already starting to build. There were a lot more people around although a lot of them had hired traditional Thai costumes to be photographed in which was all quite entertaining. By the time we had gone round the temple again, it was time to leave.

The company that runs the public ferries along the river had stopped them calling at Wat Arun, something that caused us a lot of confusion last year. The same company, however, runs tourist ferries which run the same route and do stop there..... and cost a lot more. This is a complete rip off for tourists. There was another company there which also ran a tourist ferry to where we wanted to go. This was not too badly priced so we got one of those along the river to the BTS station at Sathorn. Not quite a traditional Bangkok river experience but we had the whole air-conditioned boat just about to ourselves and it was a lot less hassle than last year.

We got the BTS to Siam and went into the Paragon shopping centre where we found a camera shop that was able to do our passport photos for us. We had to wait about 25 minutes to get them but we now have just about everything we need to get into Laos and Cambodia. Once we had collected the photos, we walked back to the Ibis.

We then had a couple of hours of sorting a few things out. Roz had to send an email regarding the sale of one of the Ruddington properties and I had to get photocopies of our passports and print out our Cambodian e-visas - all of which took quite bit of time. In the middle of doing all this, we moved from The Ibis to The Mercure. Both hotels are in the same building but again, it all took longer than it should have done.

By the time this was all sorted out, it was about 3:30.... and we were planning on going out at 6. Roz went to the pool but I decided to go straight to the gym so I could go for a run and have time to cool down again.

At 6, we walked to Feat Lab. This craft beer bar had not changed at all since we were here at Christmas 2018. Although it is relatively expensive, it was very enjoyable to have a few good pints of beer and it was easy to see how we got so pissed the last time we were there. After five pints between us, we walked back to the MBK where we only had to wait a few minutes for a table at Ban Khun Mae. We had a bottle of Sang Som with the meal and then bought another when we got back to the hotel. We drank the second bottle in the room before getting to bed at around 11:30

Thursday 30th January Roz went out to buy chocolate buns for breakfast and then went up to the pool. I went back to the Paragon shopping centre to change some Euros into Baht and then spent half an hour or so wandering round the Siam Square area. We met back in the hotel at 11:45 and went back to the MBK food hall for lunch.

Back at the hotel, we both had an hour or so by the pool and then started getting ready to leave. We had managed to get a late check-out until 4pm as we were due to leave Bangkok by train at 19:25. Just before 4, we went down and got our welcome drinks before checking out. We had our drinks and then ordered a Grab taxi to take us to the train station. The traffic was heavy and the five mile journey took 37 minutes but we were still at the station more than two hours before we could board the train.

This was the first time we had used the new "Krung Thep Aphiwat Central Terminal Station" which is now the departure point for all long distance trains from Bangkok. Inevitably, it was nowhere near as interesting a place to wait as the old station but it was comfortable enough with plenty of places to sit and the time there went reasonably quickly.

We had booked 2nd class air-con sleeping compartments on the 19:25 train to Ubon Ratchathani. Boarding started at 19:05. We think it is about 25 - 30 years since we have been on one of these trains but they do not seem to have changed much in that time. We found our seats and the train pulled out of the station on time. Not long after we had departed (just after we had finished our pot noodles) they came and made up the beds. Once the beds were made, Roz went up to her bunk and I read until I finished my book at about 11.

Friday 31st January The beds were pretty comfortable but the train was quite noisy and I woke up a lot in the night but still managed to get 4 or 5 hours sleep. I was fully awake by about 4 and Roz woke up an hour or so later. The train got into Ubon Rachtchathani a few minutes after the scheduled arrival time of 6:15.

We had a few options of how to get to our next destination - Pakse in Laos. We knew there were buses that would take us all the way but the bus station was about 10km from the train station and we would have to wait at least a couple of hours in the hope that we could get on the 9:30 bus. In the end we ordered a Grab taxi to take us to the border. This was a journey of about 90km. The taxi turned up immediately and the driver told us to cancel the Grab booking and he would take us for ฿600. This was a very good value journey and we were at the border as it opened at 8.00.

We had to buy Laos visas but we were the only ones doing this so it did not take long. We got through immigration, changed some money into Kip and then started looking at options for getting to Pakse. Eventually someone came and offered to take us for ฿200 each. We thought this would be in a private car so we accepted the offer. In the end, it was a shared minivan so a bit overpriced but it got us into Pakse by about 9:15 and we had done the whole journey from the station for less than £25 which we were more than happy with.

We crossed over the Mekong into Pakse and we got out of the van at its first stop. We think he would have driven us to our hotel but he did not know where it was and we were only 500m away so decided to walk. After breakfast from 7-Eleven again, we walked to Le Jardin Hotel. They told us to wait by the pool and by about 11 we had the key to our room. It was another very nice room in an old colonial style building. Ideal for our one night here.

We spent an hour or so by the pool or in the room and went out for a look around just before 1. We are staying a bit out of town but we are close to the Talat Dao Heuang market which is second on the list of sights in Pakse according to Lonely Planet. The market was very big but not that interesting compared to some markets we have seen particularly as a lot of the fruit and veg stalls were shutting down for the day. We bought a couple of baguettes containing some sort of unidentifiable meat and returned to the hotel to eat them. They tasted better than they looked.

After lunch, Roz went back to the pool and I fell asleep in the room. We went out again at 3:15. The main reason we had decided to stay in Pakse for a night is that I had seen pictures of Wat Phousalao and was quite keen to visit. It was a walk of just over a mile over The Mekong to get to the base of the hill that the temple is on followed by a walk up a very steep flight of steps.

At the top there is a single large Buddha statue looking out over The Mekong but it is all the smaller Buddhas behind it that I was keen on seeing. The picture I had seen had shown about 15 or so but there were hundreds of them in rows on the side of the hill. The flight left just about on time and we landed at KLIA at about 19:30. There were long queues at immigration again but for some reason, one of the queues was completely empty. We had already filled in our on-line forms so walked straight up to the desks, got our passports stamped and were checking into the Sama Sama Hotel by 20:00.

We got into our room, had showers and walked back into the airport to eat. We managed to find the food court this year and had very good curries before returning to the hotel for an early night.

Wednesday 19th February We were up just after 6 and checked out of the hotel by 6:30. We walked to the bus station so we could have a final breakfast of roti canai. We were in the terminal just after 7. Our bags were checked in all the way through from Phuket to London and we already had our boarding passes so we just had to get through immigration before getting the bus out to the satellite. All as chaotic as usual at KLIA but we were at the gate with plenty of time.

We were on exactly the same flight home as last year. We left just after the scheduled time of 9:25. The flight was not full again so we had three seats in the middle of the plane between us. We had a couple of glasses of wine after the first meal but that was it. A fairly uneventful flight of just under 14 hours.

We landed at Heathrow at 4ish. It took a while to get to the gate and to get our luggage but everything else was fairly smooth including the return of the car. Their were delays on the M25 to get onto the M3 and we stopped to buy a few things in Basingstoke. Back in Kingsclere just before 7.
