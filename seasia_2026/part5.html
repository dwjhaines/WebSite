S. E. Asia 2026

Part 5 - Phuket

Tuesday 17th February We had an excellent breakfast and spent the rest of the morning at the pool. We had arranged for a late check-out and left the hotel at 12:45. We walked over the bridge to the airport and went through into departures. The Air Asia flight to Phuket was scheduled for 14:45. They announced that it was going to be 20 minutes late but it did not actually take-off until 15:45. We landed at a very wet Phuket just after 17:00.

We had messaged the taxi driver that we had used a couple of weeks ago and he picked us up within about 10 minutes of us getting through the arrivals area. He took us back to Isara Resort where we collected our luggage and then to Nai Yang Beach Resort where we are staying for the next week. We unpacked everything and sorted out our dive gear for tomorrow. We went out and bought a few drinks (also for tomorrow) and then went and bought take-away shrimp fried rice which we ate on the balcony

Wednesday 18th February After the live-aboard we decided that we should do another couple of days diving before going home. Instead of just doing the local dive trips from Nai Yang we wanted to go back to some of the Phuket/Phi-Phi sites again. We have not dived in this area for about 10 years. Doing them from Nai Yang is not ideal as we are the wrong end of Phuket but when we got back into Thailand last week, we booked a couple of three dive day trips anyway. We will dive with Sea Bees again on Friday but today we are with a new operator for us - Aussie Divers.

We were picked up on time at 6:30 and were driven to the Aussie Divers office at Chalong Pier, a journey of about 1 hour 25 minutes. We met our dive guide, Franck and paid for the days diving before walking down to the pier. We were taken by bus along the pier to where we could board the boat. There were quite a few other customers today but the boat was not full and we had Franck to ourselves. After breakfast and a couple of briefings, the first dive was at 9:40 at Koh Doc Mai.

The first dive was not great and we did not see a lot. The conditions were quite rough and the vis was not great but we did see a few nudibranchs which we did not see many of in The Similans.

After an hours surface interval, we were in the water again by 11:40. The second dive was at Anemone Reef and was a lot better than the first. The vis was a lot better and the predicted strong currents never really happened. There were also a phenomenal number of fish all over the dive site. There were several schools of barracuda and as well as all the usual reef fish. By far the best dive of the day.

After a very good lunch, the third dive was at Shark Point. This was better than the first but no-where near the number of fish that we had seen at Anemone Reef. Still an enjoyable enough dive to complete a good days diving.

We were back at the pier before 4:30 and had a reverse of this mornings procedure. By 4:35, we were in the same minibus that we had been in this morning and were on our way back to Nai Yang. Despite the heavy traffic, we were back at the hotel by 6pm. We were both quite impressed with Aussie Divers. Everything was done very efficiently and the fact that we had our own guide was a big bonus. Would happily dive with them again.

After sorting out our gear and showering, it was a typical Nai Yang session. We had a few on the balcony before waking to Rotcharin Seafood. They seem to have started closing at 9pm so we went to Bank Restaurant instead. The food here has been very good this year and we had an enjoyable meal with a couple of bottles of Sang Som. Had a few more on the balcony before bed.

Thursday 19th February We were not feeling too bad this morning despite the amount of Sang Som consumed last night. We went for breakfast which had not changed too much despite the newly modernised restaurant. We then went to Sea Bees to pay for tomorrows trip and to see if they could repair Roz's regs which had developed a fault yesterday. We left the regs with Michael and returned to the hotel.

We did not do too much for the rest of the day apart from sitting by the pool or editing photos from The Similans. Had chocolate/banana pancakes for lunch. We have another early start tomorrow so had take-away fried rice again instead of going out in the evening.

Friday 20th February We were up at 5:30 and at the Sea Bees office before 6:00. It was an earlier start as we were supposed to be picking up two other customers in Nai Thon but they pulled out at the last minute so were taken straight to Chalong. There was less traffic at this time of morning so we were at the See Beas office in Chalong by 7:00.

We met our dive guide, Sojn and waited around for the best part of an hour while the other customers turned up. There were 48 customers on the boat today including snorkellers. We were taken down to the pier on the Sea Bees bus and then down to the boat. We left just after 8:00

The weather today was not great. It was very windy as we left Phuket meaning big waves. This was not good news as we were going to Phi Phi which was at least three hours away. It would also have an effect on which sites we could dive. We eventually arrived at Phi Phi at 11:30 by which time we were ready to jump in straight away. We were diving Gareth and Marina in our group of four.

First dive of the day was at Turtle Rock. We have dived here before with various differer names but it has usually been quite good for seeing turtles so we were quite pleased with the choice. As it was, we did not see any and it was not a great dive. We had lunch straight after the dive and then moved to Koh Bida Nok for Dive 2.

This was a much better dive. The vis was much better and there were a lot of big schools of snapper and barracuda all over the site. Towards the end of the dive we saw a hawksbill turtle which stayed with us for a few minutes and I also saw a black-tip reef shark as we were heading towards the safety stop. When everyone was back on the boat, we started heading back towards Phuket.

The third dive of the day was supposed to be at Shark Point. However, it was still too rough by the time we got back to this area so we had to dive Koh Doc Mai instead. This was a very poor dive. The vis was terrible and we managed to lose Gareth and Marina within minutes of getting in the water despite the fact that we had a second guide with us by this dive (Sojn's wife). It took us nearly 10 minutes just to find the reef and we did not really see anything of note while on the dive.

By the time everyone was out of the water, it was well past 6. We were back in Chalong at about 7:45 and were in our taxi back to Nai Yang just after 8. We eventually got back to the hotel at about 9:25. It has been quite an enjoyable day but a very long day for one good dive. Probably not a trip that we will ever repeat!

Back in the room, we sorted out some of the gear, had showers and went to 7-Eleven to get something to eat. Got to bed by about 10:30.

Saturday 21st February A quiet day with not a lot to do. Roz got up early and went for a swim. When she returned, we went to breakfast. She then had an hour or so at the pool while I sorted out photos from the last few days. Just after 10, we went round to Sea Bees, washed out our BCDs and returned our bags and weight belts.

With the exception of a trip out to buy banana pancakes, the rest of the day was spent by the pool.

We started drinking while watching the first half of the Swansea v Bristol City game and went out at about 8:45. We had another very good meal at Bank restaurant and then returned to the room to chat to our Russian neighbour and watch the Curling from the Winter Olympics.

Sunday 22nd February A similar day to yesterday. i.e. we did not do a lot except for sitting by the pool. It was also a similar evening. We had considered going to a bar to watch Sunderland v Fulham but were both quite content at Bank restaurant so watched it there on my phone instead.

Monday 23rd February Another day of not doing a lot. Breakfast, pool, pancakes on the balcony and then back to the pool for the afternoon.

We did go to the beach in the evening to look at the sunset. However, in the time between us leaving the pool and getting to the beach, it had completely clouded over so we gave up on that idea. Roz went and had her nails panted instead.

At 7, we started on our final session of this visit to Thailand. It was pretty much the same as the last two evenings. Drinks on the balcony followed by a couple of hours at Bank Restaurant with two bottles of Sang Som. Another very enjoyable night.
