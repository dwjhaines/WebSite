Valencia & Madrid

Spain v England. Nov 2026.

Thursday 12th November - England v Croatia We started the trip with a visit to Wembley. We got the 10:00 bus from Kingsclere and then the 10:35 train from Basingstoke to Clapham Junction. From there, we got another train to West Brompton and then walked to the Ibis Hotel. We were too early to get into our room so had our welcome drink in the bar while we waited. By 2pm, we had checked in, left our stuff in the room and were on our way out for the day.

Mark and decided not to come today and Steve was not going to The Doric Arch so we had a few hours to kill before meeting him. We started off by walking to Walham Green, a very nice new Wetherspoons next to Fulham Broadway station. We had lunch and a couple of drinks in there before getting the underground to Paddington. We had a couple of drinks in The Bear Pub (a pub we had not been in before) and a couple more in The Pride of Paddington (a pub I had not been into for about 35 years).

We got the underground up to Wembley by about 6:30 and met Steve, Mark and Rob in JJ Moons. We had time for one drink in there before walking to the ground. The game was not great but the win meant that England are definitely qualified for the knock-out stages. We had a couple more drinks in JJ Moons after the game and hten got the train back to West Brompton.

Friday 13th November We left the hotel at about 8:30 and got the underground to Liverpool Street. We bought breakfast at the station and caught the 9:35 train to Stansted Airport. We went straight through into the departures area and had an hour or so to kill before the 12:30 Ryanair flight to Valencia. As usual, we were sat in different parts of the plane but it left on time and we were in Valencia and through the airport just after 16:00 hours. We got the metro into the centre and checked into the SH Colón hotel at about 17:00.

After showers and were about to go out when Roz had an email telling her that she had got a ticket for Sunday's game through the waiting list.I did not get one. after she had paid for the ticket, we headed out for the evening. We walked to the area near the cathedral and started with a couple of drinks in Taberna Vintara, the bar we had been in after Swansea's win in Valencia 13 years ago. We then started walking in the direction of another bar we had been in at that time. We didn't find it but did find a few more decent places on the way. We ended up in Bar La Pilareta where we stood at the bar drinking cheap white win for an hour or so before getting an Uber back to the hotel.
