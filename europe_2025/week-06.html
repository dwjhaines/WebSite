Week 6

Sunday 6th July I was up at 7:00 and walked into town to buy bread rolls for lunch. We had breakfast in the hotel restaurant before returning to the room to start packing up our things. We will be leaving the car in Sigmaringen so had to sort out what to put in the panniers and what to leave here.

We checked out just before 10:00. We left the car parked outside the hotel and cycled back to the station again. The plan today was to get the same train that we had tried to get on yesterday but this time to Tuttlingen. Today everything went a bit more smoothly and the train left on time at 10:34. We arrived in Tuttlingen about 45 minutes later and cycled down to the Donauradweg again.

We cycled up-river to Donaueschingen, the source of the Danube, a journey of about 23 miles.The scenery today was not as spectacular as yesterday. It is all a bit confusing about how The Danube splits and disappears underground at one point and yet continues to flow further down but still all quite interesting.We got to a point where the river was totally dry and also went to look at the point where the two rivers join to form the start of The Danube.

We cycled into Donaueschingen and went to the starting point of the Donauradweg. We now only have a section of about 10km to complete before we will hav edone the whole route. We had a quick look at the spring, also considered the start of The Danube but could not really get down to look at this properly with our bikes. We then had a ride of about a mile and a half to get to where we staying - Pension Jägerhaus. It is a bit of a way out of the town and up a hill but we have a very nice apartment again.
